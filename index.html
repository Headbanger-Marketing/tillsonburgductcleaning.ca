FAQs

Questions Tillsonburg Homeowners Ask

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

How often should ducts be cleaned around Tillsonburg?

In town, two to three years is typical. Surrounded by the fields, sandy soils and corn season argue for every second year. Dust that reappears within days of wiping means the returns are calling.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, certified and fully insured on every job. Ontario issues no trade licence for duct cleaning, so the credentials that protect you are certification, training, and insurance, and all three stand behind our crews.

What proof do you leave that the job was done?

Photos of your duct interiors from before and after the work, reviewed with you before we leave the property. You should never have to take a duct cleaner's word for anything.

Our dryer is hot but slow. Machine or vent?

The vent, nearly always. Long paths and heavy laundry loads load lint until airflow chokes. We clear the pipe end to end and measure the flow at the hood, and if the machine itself looks like the problem, we say so and save you the visit.

What is involved, and how long does it take?

Negative-pressure equipment connects to the system, registers come off and get washed, each branch is brushed under suction, the blower compartment is cleaned, and photos document it all. Tillsonburg homes generally take three to five hours.

Can you clear the musty smell from the vents?

Yes. Cleaning removes the residue holding the odour, then a sanitizer application to the clean duct surfaces addresses what remains. In that order the result holds.

Why does the house dust over again so soon?

Usually return-side leakage, drawing unfiltered air from the cellar or cavities into the fan. We inspect and seal for that specifically, which lasts far longer than another cleaning.

Where do you serve beyond town?

Tillsonburg plus Courtland, Otterville, Norwich, Ingersoll, and toward Simcoe and Delhi. Outlying farm addresses are regular work for us, just confirm at booking.
